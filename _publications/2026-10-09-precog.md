PreQA diagnoses unmet visual requirements from low-cost previews. PreEdit contrasts predictions under matched target and counterfactual conditions to estimate a correction direction, then applies blockwise soft projection to source-relative trajectory updates to attenuate unrelated changes. Cached generation states and velocities support editing and continuation, and only the selected candidate receives full rendering.
